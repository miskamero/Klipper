MATERIALS AND INTEGRITY

dependency-provenance.json pins the media binaries. compliance/inventory.json
lists collected dependency versions, license expressions, archive hashes,
upstream references and unresolved obligations. Verbatim notices/license texts
are in compliance/licenses/; original source archives are in compliance/sources/.
compliance/manifest.json records their SHA-256 hashes and binds the collection
to the binary pins and package lockfiles. Both installer and ZIP include these
same materials. Copyright headers remain intact in the supplied source archives.

WINDOWS PREREQUISITES

Windows system DLLs and graphics drivers are not redistributed. The ZIP needs
WebView2 already installed. The installer may download Microsoft's WebView2
bootstrapper during installation; this is a separately licensed prerequisite,
not an included Klipper source component. No developer toolchains are shipped.
